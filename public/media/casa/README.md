## Quality rules
- Prefer real CASA moments (students, teachers, city activities, tandem, excursions).
- Keep enough negative space for headline overlays.
- Avoid heavy compression artifacts.
- Use filenames with lowercase, hyphen-separated words.

## Authenticity rules
- Treat these as historical CASA photos unless a current source confirms otherwise.
- Do not use old staff group photos for current team claims without human approval.
- Do not imply a specific accommodation room is currently available unless the current housing offer verifies it.
- Do not pair a recognizable student portrait with a different named testimonial.
- Do not publish generated or person-specific portrait assets for verified real staff/team/testimonial claims.
- Synthetic placeholder portraits may be used only as temporary design placeholders and must be documented as synthetic.
- Keep edits source-faithful: crop, resize, light exposure/color correction only.
